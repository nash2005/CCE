Terms and Conditions

Welcome to the School Online Requisition System of the City College of El Salvador. By accessing and using this system, you agree to the following terms and conditions. Please read them carefully before proceeding.

1. System Purpose

This system is designed exclusively for the online requesting and processing of academic documents, including but not limited to Transcript of Records (TOR), Certifications, Diplomas, Clearances, and Certificate of Authenticity and Verification (CAV). It is intended solely for enrolled students, alumni, and authorized personnel of the City College of El Salvador.

2. User Accounts and Access

Each user is provided a unique account by the institution. You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your login credentials. Sharing your account with others is strictly prohibited. Any activity performed under your account is your responsibility. Report any unauthorized access to the Registrar's Office immediately.

3. Accuracy of Information

You agree to provide accurate and truthful information when submitting document requests. Any falsification of records or misrepresentation of personal data may result in the denial of your request and may be subject to disciplinary action in accordance with the City College of El Salvador's Student Handbook and applicable laws.

4. Document Request Processing

All document requests are subject to verification and approval by the designated offices, including the Dean's Office, Library, Finance Office, and Guidance Office. Processing times may vary depending on the type of document requested and current workload. The institution reserves the right to reject requests that are incomplete, inaccurate, or non-compliant with institutional policies.

5. Fees and Payments

Certain documents may require payment of corresponding fees as set by the City College of El Salvador. Fees must be settled at the Finance Office or through approved payment channels before documents are released. Fee schedules are subject to change without prior notice.

6. Clearance Requirements

Some document requests require clearance from multiple departments (Dean's Office, Library, Finance, and Guidance). Your request will only proceed to the next stage once all required clearances have been obtained. It is your responsibility to settle any outstanding obligations with each department.

7. Data Privacy

Your personal data collected through this system will be used solely for academic and administrative purposes. We will not share your information with third parties without your consent, except as required by law.
